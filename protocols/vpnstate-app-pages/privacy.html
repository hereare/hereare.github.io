VPNState Privacy Policy

简体中文

1. 提供方与适用范围

VPNState 由 Zhaochen Ren 提供。本政策适用于 VPNState App 及其小组件、实时活动和快捷指令动作。产品支持：VPNState 支持页面。

2. 我们不收集或上传的内容

VPNState 不要求账号，不销售个人数据，不做跨 App 追踪，不集成广告或开发者控制的第三方分析 SDK。App 不把你的状态记录上传到开发者服务器，不读取或代理 VPN 流量、网页浏览内容、VPN 密码或订阅凭据。

3. 保存在设备上的内容

  • 通过快捷指令记录的开启、关闭或未知状态，记录时间、来源和历史；
  • 显示偏好、实时活动开关、引导步骤与手动确认的配置进度；
  • 你填写的锁屏自定义文本；
  • 若从内部测试版本更新，可能保留该版本生成的本地校验历史与偏好。

上述内容保存在 App 沙盒或本机 App Group 容器，用于主 App、小组件和快捷指令协作。App Store 与 TestFlight 版本不会自动读取或校验系统 VPN 状态。内部开发／Ad Hoc 版本可能提供本机状态检测与诊断；相关数据仍仅在设备上保存。

4. 系统功能与锁屏可见性

VPNState 不提供 VPN 服务，不替你连接或断开 VPN。你选择的 VPN App、Apple 快捷指令、WidgetKit 和实时活动由相应提供方与系统处理。状态与自定义文本可能显示在锁屏或灵动岛，其他人可能看到；请避免填写敏感信息，并按需调整系统锁屏权限。系统组件刷新和实时活动时长由 iOS 决定。

5. 外部页面与主动反馈

隐私政策和支持页面托管于 GitHub Pages，不含广告或开发者控制的分析脚本。GitHub 可能按其政策处理网页访问日志。打开外部网页时由浏览器处理，VPNState 不会把本地状态记录附加到链接中。

如果你主动通过 GitHub Issues 反馈，你发布的内容可能公开。请不要提交 VPN 账号、订阅链接、令牌、私人线路信息、个人联系方式或未遮挡的敏感截图。

6. 删除、备份和联系

你可以用「设置锁屏自定义文本」动作留空清除文字，关闭实时活动、移除组件，或按 iOS 的管理方式删除 App。共享容器清理和设备／iCloud 备份由系统管理；备份中的副本遵循你的系统设置。开发者没有这些本地记录的服务器副本。

产品或隐私问题，请通过支持页面联系。政策发生变化时会更新本页日期。

English

1. Provider and scope

VPNState is provided by Zhaochen Ren. This policy covers the app, widgets, Live Activities, and Shortcuts actions. Visit VPNState Support for help.

2. Data we do not collect or upload

VPNState requires no account, sells no personal data, does not track you across apps, and includes no advertising or developer-controlled third-party analytics SDKs. It does not upload your status records to developer servers or read or proxy VPN traffic, browsing content, VPN passwords, or subscription credentials.

3. Data on your device

  • On, off, or unknown status reported through Shortcuts, with time, source, and history;
  • Display preferences, the Live Activity preference, onboarding steps, and manually confirmed setup progress;
  • Your custom Lock Screen text;
  • Local verification history and preferences retained from an internal test version, if applicable.

These remain in the app sandbox or a local App Group container for use by the app, its widgets, and Shortcuts actions. App Store and TestFlight builds do not automatically read or verify system VPN status. Internal development and Ad Hoc builds may offer local status diagnostics; those records also remain on the device.

4. System features and visibility

VPNState is not a VPN service and does not connect or disconnect a VPN. Your VPN app, Apple Shortcuts, WidgetKit, and Live Activities operate under their respective providers and system settings. Status and custom text may be visible to others on the Lock Screen or Dynamic Island. Avoid sensitive text and adjust system permissions as needed. iOS controls refresh timing and Live Activity duration.

5. External pages and feedback

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages without advertising or developer-controlled analytics scripts. GitHub may handle website access logs under its policies. VPNState does not attach local status records to external links.

Information you voluntarily post to GitHub Issues may be public. Do not include VPN credentials, subscription links, tokens, private routing details, personal contact information, or unredacted sensitive screenshots.

6. Deletion, backups, and contact

Run the custom text action with an empty value to clear that text. You can disable Live Activities, remove widgets, or delete the app using iOS. Shared container cleanup and device or iCloud backups are managed by the system; backup copies follow your settings. The developer holds no server-side copy of these local records.

For product or privacy questions, visit Support. Changes to this policy will update the date above.
