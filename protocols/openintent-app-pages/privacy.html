OpenIntent Privacy Policy. Effective date: June 12, 2026. Last updated: June 12, 2026.

有意打开 is an iOS app that helps users intentionally lock and unlock selected apps with Apple Screen Time, FamilyControls, ManagedSettings, and ManagedSettingsUI. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it stays, and how support requests are handled.

No account.
No sign-in or user profile is required.

Local-first.

1. Who Provides the App

The app is provided by Zhaochen Ren. For support, use the public support page: 有意打开 Support.

2. Data We Do Not Collect From the App

有意打开 does not collect personal data from the app. In particular, the app does not collect or upload:

  • name, email address, phone number, account credentials, or contact lists;
  • advertising identifiers, device identifiers for tracking, or cross-app tracking data;
  • analytics events, crash reports, browsing history, precise location, photos, or files;
  • a server-side copy of the apps, categories, or web domains selected in Screen Time.

3. Local Data Used by the App

To provide its core functionality, the app stores limited configuration data locally on your device, including:

  • protection group names that you create;
  • lock/unlock state and group settings;
  • Apple-provided FamilyControls selection tokens for selected apps, categories, or web domains;
  • whether the in-app guide has been seen.

This data is stored in the app sandbox and shared app group container so the main app, Shield Action extension, and Shield Configuration extension can work together. It is not sent to a developer-operated server.

4. Apple Screen Time and FamilyControls

App selection and Screen Time authorization are handled by Apple system frameworks. 有意打开 receives system tokens needed to apply the protection you request. These tokens are used locally with ManagedSettings to lock or unlock selected apps and categories.

You can revoke Screen Time authorization in iOS Settings. If authorization is revoked, features that depend on FamilyControls may stop working until permission is granted again.

5. Support Website and GitHub Issues

This privacy policy and support page are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard web access logs according to GitHub's own terms and privacy practices. These pages do not include third-party analytics scripts or advertising scripts controlled by the app developer.

If you open a support request on GitHub Issues, the information you choose to post may be public. Do not include sensitive app lists, private screenshots, personal contact details, or other confidential information in a public issue.

6. How Data Is Used

Local configuration data is used only to provide app protection features that you request: showing groups, applying shields, allowing intentional unlocks, restoring lock state, and showing the shield UI.

Support information you voluntarily provide through GitHub Issues is used to investigate bugs, answer questions, and improve documentation.

7. Sharing, Sale, and Advertising

有意打开 does not sell personal data, does not share app data with advertisers or data brokers, and does not use advertising networks. The app does not include a developer-operated backend for collecting user data.

8. Retention and Deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you change or delete groups, reset app settings where available, revoke relevant permissions, or delete the app. Deleting the app removes the app's local sandboxed data according to iOS behavior.

GitHub Issues content is controlled through GitHub. If you want a support issue edited or removed, request it in the issue thread or open a new support request.

9. Security

The app relies on iOS sandboxing, app group access controls, and Apple entitlement checks. No Internet transmission of app selection tokens is performed by the app. No system can be guaranteed completely secure, so avoid posting sensitive details in public support channels.

10. Children's Privacy

The app is not designed to collect personal data from children. The app uses Apple Screen Time and FamilyControls only after system authorization. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a public support request, contact support so the public content can be reviewed.

11. Your Choices

  • Do not grant Screen Time authorization if you do not want the app to manage selected app shields.
  • Remove selected apps or delete protection groups in the app to stop using those selections.
  • Delete the app to remove local app data from the device according to iOS behavior.
  • Use GitHub controls for content you choose to post in support issues.

12. Changes

This policy may be updated when the app's features, data handling, or support channels change. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
