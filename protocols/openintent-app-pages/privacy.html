有意打开 Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 12, 2026

Overview

有意打开 helps users intentionally lock and unlock selected apps with Apple Screen Time, FamilyControls, ManagedSettings, and ManagedSettingsUI. The app is designed to work locally on the user's device.

Data Collection

有意打开 does not collect personal data from users. The app does not use accounts, advertising identifiers, third-party analytics SDKs, or tracking SDKs.

Local Data

The app may store app protection groups, user-selected settings, and Apple-provided Screen Time / FamilyControls tokens locally on the device or in the app group container. These values are used only to provide the app locking and unlocking features.

Apple Screen Time and FamilyControls

App selection and related Screen Time authorization are handled by Apple system frameworks. 有意打开 does not receive a plain list of personal app usage from a server and does not upload Screen Time selections to a developer server.

Data Sharing

有意打开 does not sell, share, or transfer user data to advertisers, data brokers, or analytics providers.
