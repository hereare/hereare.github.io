简体中文

1. 提供方

本 App 由 Zhaochen Ren 提供。支持页面：有意打开 Support。

2. 我们不收集的内容

有意打开不收集个人数据，不销售数据，不做跨 App 追踪，也不包含广告网络或开发者控制的分析 SDK。App 不会收集或上传：

  • 姓名、邮箱、电话、账号凭据、通讯录；
  • 广告标识符、用于追踪的设备标识符、跨 App 追踪数据；
  • 使用分析事件、浏览历史、精确位置、照片或文件；
  • 你在 Screen Time 选择器里选择的具体 App、类别或网站清单。

3. App 本地使用的数据

为了提供加锁、解锁、快捷指令和拦截页确认等功能，App 会在本机保存少量配置：

  • 你创建的保护分组名称；
  • 分组的加锁状态、解锁状态、紧急全部解锁状态和相关设置；
  • 是否允许在 App 拦截页解锁当前 App 所在分组并进入；
  • 是否允许 Shortcuts 动作执行分组加锁或解锁；
  • 系统 FamilyControls 提供的选择 token；
  • 使用引导是否已显示等本地界面状态。

这些数据保存在 App 沙盒和 App Group 容器中，用于主 App、Shield Action extension 和 Shield Configuration extension 协同工作。App 本身不联网，不会把这些数据发送到开发者服务器。

4. Screen Time、FamilyControls 和 Shortcuts

App 选择和 Screen Time 授权由 Apple 系统框架处理。有意打开只接收系统提供的 token，用于本地调用 ManagedSettings 加锁或解锁对应分组。由于 token 是系统抽象标识，App 本身无法获得你选择的具体 App 信息。

Shortcuts 动作，例如 Lock App Group 和 Unlock App Group，用于在本机执行你设置的分组操作。快捷指令可把“解锁并打开 App”和“关闭后重新加锁”串起来。

5. 支持网站和 GitHub Issues

本隐私政策和支持页面托管在 GitHub Pages。GitHub 可能按自己的条款处理标准网页访问日志。这些页面不包含由 App 开发者控制的第三方分析脚本或广告脚本。

如果你通过 GitHub Issues 提交支持请求，你主动发布的信息可能是公开的。请不要在公开 issue 中包含私密 App 列表、个人联系方式、私密截图或敏感家庭/设备信息。

6. 删除和选择

本地数据会保留在你的设备上，直到你修改或删除分组、撤销权限、重置相关设置或删除 App。删除 App 会按 iOS 行为移除 App 沙盒内的数据。

  • 如果不希望 App 管理 App 拦截，请不要授予 Screen Time 权限。
  • 你可以从分组中移除选择，或删除分组来停止对应保护。
  • 你可以在 iOS 设置中撤销 Screen Time 授权。

7. 联系方式

产品支持或隐私问题，请访问 有意打开 Support。

English

1. Provider

The app is provided by Zhaochen Ren. For support, visit OpenIntent Support.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

OpenIntent does not collect personal data from the app, does not sell data, does not track you across apps, and does not include advertising networks or developer-controlled analytics SDKs. The app does not collect or upload:

  • name, email address, phone number, account credentials, or contact lists;
  • advertising identifiers, device identifiers for tracking, or cross-app tracking data;
  • analytics events, browsing history, precise location, photos, or files;
  • a server-side copy of the specific apps, categories, or websites selected in Screen Time.

3. Local Data Used by the App

To provide group locking, unlocking, Shortcuts actions, and optional shield-page confirmation, the app stores limited configuration locally on your device:

  • protected group names you create;
  • group lock state, unlock state, emergency unlock state, and related settings;
  • whether entry from the app shield is allowed for a protected group;
  • whether Shortcuts actions are allowed to lock or unlock a group;
  • Apple-provided FamilyControls selection tokens;
  • local interface state, such as whether the guide has been shown.

This data is stored in the app sandbox and shared app group container so the main app, Shield Action extension, and Shield Configuration extension can work together. The app itself does not connect to the network and does not send this data to a developer-operated server.

4. Screen Time, FamilyControls, and Shortcuts

App selection and Screen Time authorization are handled by Apple system frameworks. OpenIntent receives Apple-provided tokens used locally with ManagedSettings to lock or unlock protected groups. Because these tokens are system abstractions, OpenIntent does not understand the specific apps you chose.

Shortcuts actions, such as Lock App Group and Unlock App Group, run locally to perform the group actions you configure. Shortcuts can be used to unlock and open an app, then lock again after the app closes.

5. Support Website and GitHub Issues

This privacy policy and support page are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard web access logs according to GitHub's own terms and privacy practices. These pages do not include third-party analytics scripts or advertising scripts controlled by the app developer.

If you open a support request on GitHub Issues, the information you choose to post may be public. Do not include private app lists, personal contact details, confidential screenshots, or sensitive family/device information in a public issue.

6. Deletion and Choices

Local app data remains on your device until you change or delete groups, revoke permissions, reset relevant settings, or delete the app. Deleting the app removes the app's sandboxed data according to iOS behavior.

  • Do not grant Screen Time authorization if you do not want the app to manage selected app shields.
  • Remove selections from a group, or delete a group, to stop using that protection.
  • Revoke Screen Time authorization in iOS Settings if needed.
