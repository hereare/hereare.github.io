Support page for 有意打开.

Getting Started

  1. Open 有意打开.
  2. Tap the Screen Time authorization button and allow access.
  3. Create a protection group and select apps to protect.
  4. Use the app or Shortcuts actions to lock and unlock a group intentionally.

Common Questions

Why does the app ask for Screen Time authorization?
The app uses Apple Screen Time and FamilyControls to let you select and protect apps on your own device.

Does the app upload my selected apps?
No. App selections and related tokens are stored locally for the locking and unlocking features.

How do I remove protection?
Open 有意打开 and unlock the relevant group, or remove the selected apps from that group.
